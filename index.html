How predictions work

This app blends age-based sleep priors with your baby’s recent wake windows and nap lengths. Recent history is weighted with an exponentially weighted moving average, a robust median steadies the model, circadian sleepy windows nudge likely nap timing, and the daytime sleep budget tapers late predictions when enough day sleep has already happened.
